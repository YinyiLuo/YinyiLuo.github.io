About Me

Hi, I’m Yinyi Luo. I’m currently a Ph.D. student at Carnegie Mellon University. My research focuses on agentic AI systems, unified multimodal models, and multimodal reasoning. I am interested in building learning systems that can integrate information across modalities, reason about complex environments, and interact with the world through tools.

I’m open to collaboration. Feel free to email me.
